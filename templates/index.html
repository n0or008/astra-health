NASA HUMAN RESEARCH PROGRAM

Mission Hazard Intelligence

RIDGE MONITOR

Five major hazards can affect astronaut health and performance during long-duration space missions.

01

Space Radiation

High-energy particles and solar events can expose astronauts to radiation beyond Earth's natural protection.

Monitoring focus Radiation exposure and dosimeter information
02

Isolation & Confinement

Living in a confined environment for long periods can affect sleep, mood, behaviour and teamwork.

Monitoring focus Sleep duration, mood and crew well-being
03

Distance from Earth

Communication delays and limited access to supplies and medical support make self-sufficiency essential.

Monitoring focus Health records and onboard medical readiness
04

Altered Gravity

Microgravity and changing gravity environments can affect bones, muscles, balance and cardiovascular function.

Monitoring focus Heart rate, blood pressure and physical health assessments
05

Hostile / Closed Environments

Spacecraft conditions such as temperature, pressure, lighting, noise and microbial exposure matter for crew health.

Monitoring focus Environmental conditions and immune health research

PREVENTIVE SUPPORT / MISSION WELLNESS

Countermeasure Guidance

NASA-informed strategies for managing common spaceflight health risks.

🏃

Bone & Muscle Health

Exercise is an important countermeasure against changes in muscle strength, bone health and cardiovascular fitness.

Monitoring focus: Exercise activity and physical performance.
🌙

Sleep & Fatigue

Consistent sleep opportunities and careful work-rest scheduling help support alertness and performance.

Monitoring focus: Sleep duration, sleep quality and fatigue reports.
🥗

Nutrition & Hydration

Nutrition is an important part of maintaining crew health during spaceflight. Follow the mission's approved nutrition plan.

Monitoring focus: Nutrition records and hydration observations.
🧠

Behavioral Wellbeing

Regular communication, team support and behavioral-health resources can help crews manage isolation and confinement.

Monitoring focus: Mood check-ins, stress reports and social connection.
☢️

Radiation Protection

Radiation exposure is monitored through mission systems and managed using approved mission protection procedures.

Monitoring focus: Radiation monitoring data and mission alerts.
🩺

Medical Monitoring

Changes in health readings or new symptoms should be documented and reviewed according to mission medical procedures.

Monitoring focus: Vital signs, symptoms and health trends.

IMPORTANT: These are educational guidance topics, not personalized medical instructions. Crew members should follow approved mission protocols and contact the flight surgeon when required.
